Photograph lunch. Get the label.

CalTrack recognises 101 dishes from a photo, then looks up what’s in them in the USDA’s FoodData Central. You pick the portion, and it adds the calories, protein, carbs and fat to your day.

When a photo is ambiguous it shows its five best guesses and says it isn’t sure, so you choose instead of it guessing for you.

It knows 101 dishes

An EfficientNet-B2 trained on the Food-101 dataset, from apple pie to waffles. For anything else, search by name.

The numbers have a source

Every dish links to the USDA record its nutrition came from. Dishes blended from several records say so.

Your photos aren’t kept

A photo is classified in memory and discarded. Only the dish and portion go into your diary.
